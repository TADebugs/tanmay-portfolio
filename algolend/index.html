LendingPool contract

NOT DEPLOYED
  • depositadds to your balance and the pool total
  • withdrawpays back the amount plus simple interest since your last update
  • borrowchecks the pool has the liquidity, then pays out
  • repayclears your borrowed balance

The rate is stored as interestRate in basis points: 500 bps is 5 %. Interest accrues over Global.latestTimestamp. Written in Algorand TypeScript and kept in the repo, not deployed, so the app simulates deposits and loans.

What was built

  • Full-stack decentralized lending platform with React, TypeScript and FastAPI (Python), integrating Algorand smart contracts with fast finality and sub-cent fees.
  • Three AI agent modules for market analysis, risk scoring and yield optimization.
